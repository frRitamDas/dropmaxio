Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. About this policy

EditMax is operated by EditMax LLC (“EditMax”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains how EditMax LLC handles personal information of visitors to dropmax.io, users of the EditMax app, and shoppers who visit or buy from stores built with EditMax.

2. Information we collect

Account and billing: your name, email, sign-in details, plan, payment status and limited payment-method info. Full card details are handled by Stripe, not stored by EditMax.

Your content: stores, products, settings, domains, uploaded images and videos, and generated content.

Usage: IP address, approximate country, device and browser information, and what you do in the app.

Anything else you send us, such as messages to support, feedback or details in a request.

2a. Shoppers on EditMax stores

When someone visits or buys from a store built with EditMax, EditMax LLC collects and processes shopper information on behalf of the store owner: name, email, phone, shipping and billing address, order details, contact-form messages, and device, IP and browsing data. Card details are collected directly by Stripe.

We use shopper information to run the store, process payments, fulfill and ship orders, prevent fraud, handle support and disputes, and keep required records. We share it with the store owner, Stripe, and fulfillment and shipping partners as needed. We do not sell shopper information.

The store owner is responsible for their own use of shopper data and may have their own privacy notice. Shoppers can email dropmaxsupport@gmail.com to exercise their privacy rights.

3. Why we use information

We use your information to make EditMax work — your account, stores, tools, subscription and support — to keep the service safe, and to keep records the law requires. Where a legal basis is needed, we rely on our contract with you, legal obligations, legitimate interests such as security, or consent you can withdraw at any time.

4. Payments and Stripe

EditMax Payments is powered by Stripe. Stripe handles payments you make to EditMax and collects your card details directly; we only see payment status and limited payment-method details.

Payouts to store owners run through Stripe Connect. Stripe processes those transfers and the account information they need under its own Privacy Policy.

5. AI and video content

The AI tools we use process your prompts and the product text or images you send when you ask for AI-assisted content. Don't submit sensitive personal information or content you have no right to share. These providers don't use your content to train their models by default, but may keep records for security and abuse monitoring under their own policies. The video studio currently uses your product's own clips; if video generation is turned on, the prompts and media you send go to that provider under its own privacy policy.

6. Who receives information

Providers see your information only as their job requires: Lovable Cloud for hosting, storage and delivery, Stripe for payments, OpenAI (and Higgsfield if switched on) for AI features, plus email, security and support tools. Public store content is visible to visitors. We may disclose information when the law requires it, to protect rights or safety, with your permission, or in a business sale. We do not sell personal information.

7. Cookies and local storage

Cookies and local storage keep you signed in, protect your account and remember preferences. You can clear them in your browser, but blocking essential storage may stop some features from working.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep information as long as needed to run the service, keep accounts secure and meet legal obligations. Email dropmaxsupport@gmail.com to delete your account; once we've verified it's you, we delete or anonymize your information and keep only records the law requires, such as tax and dispute records.

9. Security and international processing

We use encrypted connections and access controls, though no service is completely secure. Providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States, using lawful transfer safeguards where required. Email us if you'd like details about those safeguards.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your information, or object to how it's used. Update account details in Settings or email dropmaxsupport@gmail.com to make a request. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

11. Children

EditMax accounts are for adults 18 or older, or the age of majority where higher. We don't knowingly collect children’s information; if you believe a child has given us theirs, email us and we'll address it.

12. Changes and contact

Email dropmaxsupport@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. We may update this policy and will give notice of material changes where required.
